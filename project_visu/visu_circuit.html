How to use

Insert a part from the menu (or press its letter), then click the canvas.
Wire: drag between two points.
Select: click to select, drag to move.
Pan: drag to pan  ·  wheel to zoom.
Right‑click anything to delete it.
Double‑click a part to edit its value.
Double‑click a switch to toggle it.
AC sources have adjustable amplitude, frequency (Hz) and phase (°).
Simulation starts paused — press Space or Run.
